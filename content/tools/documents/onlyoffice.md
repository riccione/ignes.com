## Key Features

* Real-time collaborative editing with comments, built-in chat, track changes, and version history.
* Full Office Open XML compatibility — DOCX, XLSX, PPTX — plus ODF, PDF, and more.
* Complete editor set: Document, Spreadsheet, Presentation, Form Creator, PDF Editor, and Diagram Viewer.
* Plugins, macros, and 50+ connectors for Nextcloud, ownCloud, Seafile, Moodle, Odoo, and others.
* Self-hosted and private with JWT authentication, file encryption, and access controls.
