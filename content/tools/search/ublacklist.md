## Key Features

* Completely block unwanted sites from appearing in search results.
* Block by URL with match patterns or expressions, or by title regular expressions.
* Highlight preferred sites in search results.
* Synchronize rulesets across devices via Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, WebDAV, or browser sync.
* Supports Google, Bing, Brave, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, Kagi, SearXNG, Startpage, Yahoo! JAPAN, and Yandex.

## Notes

uBlacklist is a results filter rather than a search engine, so Kagi is only a partial paid analogue for its site-blocking and personalization features.
