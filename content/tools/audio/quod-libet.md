## Key Features

* Playlists and searches based on regular expressions.
* Display and edit any tag, across all supported file formats.
* Scales to libraries with tens of thousands of songs.
* ReplayGain, podcasts, internet radio, and album art.
* Over 90 plugins, plus Ex Falso, a standalone tag editor.
