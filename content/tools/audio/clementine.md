## Key Features

* Search and play your local music library.
* Internet radio from SomaFM, Radio-Browser.info, Icecast, and more.
* Smart and dynamic playlists with tag editing and MusicBrainz lookup.
* Podcasts via gpodder.net and Last.fm scrobbling.
* Equalizer, moodbar, projectM visualisations, and remote control apps.

## Project Status

Clementine was dormant from 2016 to 2024 and has been actively maintained again since the 1.4.1 release, though its open-issue backlog remains large.
