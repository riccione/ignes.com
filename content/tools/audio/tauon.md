## Key Features

* Fast, responsive interface with automatic theming.
* MilkDrop visualisations powered by projectM.
* Gapless playback of FLAC, Opus, WavPack, APE, DSD, CUE, and MOD.
* Stream from Plex, Jellyfin, and Airsonic/Subsonic servers, with TIDAL integration.
* Customisable widget layouts, smart playlists, synced lyrics, and MusicBrainz fingerprint lookup.
