## Key Features

* Plays almost all audio/video formats without external codecs.
* Streaming media support (HTTP, RTSP, MMS, torrent streaming).
* Hardware-accelerated decoding (VDPAU, VA-API, VideoToolbox, DXVA).
* Subtitle and chapter support with automatic subtitle detection.
