## Key Features

* Fully customisable widget-based interface, from a blank canvas or preset layouts.
* Library management with standard playlists, autoplaylists, and advanced filtering.
* Major formats, archive playback, CUE sheets, internet radio, and CD ripping on Linux.
* Plugin system for widgets, decoders, tag readers, and DSPs, plus FooScript.
* ReplayGain, MusicBrainz metadata, AccurateRip, lyrics, and scrobbling.
