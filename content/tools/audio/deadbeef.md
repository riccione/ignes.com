## Key Features

* Plays MP3, FLAC, APE, WavPack, M4A, MPC, chiptunes, and tracker modules via FFmpeg.
* Reads and writes ID3, APEv2, and VorbisComments with a high-quality tag editor.
* CUE sheet support, embedded or external, with automatic album splitting.
* 18-band equalizer, DSP pipeline, gapless playback, ReplayGain, and audio converter.
