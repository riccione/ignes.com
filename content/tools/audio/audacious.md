## Key Features

* Lightweight with low system resource usage.
* Wide audio format support (MP3, FLAC, AAC, Opus, Vorbis, and more).
* Multiple UIs: Qt, GTK, and Winamp Classic skins.
* Advanced playlist management, graphical equalizer, and gapless playback.
