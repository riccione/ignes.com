## Key Features

* Personal recommendations, trends, tags, and recent releases.
* Scheduled flows, discover playlists, and imports from Spotify, YouTube Music, and Last.fm.
* Integrations with Lidarr, Navidrome, Plex, Jellyfin, yt-dlp, and 17 more.
* Multi-user profiles with local, OIDC, Google, or Plex sign-in.
