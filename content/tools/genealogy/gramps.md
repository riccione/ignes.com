## Key Features

* Research views for people, families, charts, events, places, and geography.
* Extensive GEDCOM 5.5.1 import/export for exchanging data with other programs.
* Custom filters and unlimited bookmarks for reports and data exports.
* Multilingual — translated into approximately 40 languages.
