## Key Features

* Automated mapping of rooms, terrain, exits, and connections in real time as you explore.
* Interactive map with fast OpenGL rendering, a pseudo-3D view, and drag-and-drop room layers.
* Group map sharing so you can see other players move on your map in real time.
* Built-in MUD client that lets you play and manage the map in one window.
* Remote or offline map editing with an external editor or the built-in one.

## Scope

MMapper is specific to the MUME MUD, so it is useful only to players of that game. It is actively maintained by a small volunteer team.
