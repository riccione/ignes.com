## Key Features

* Accounting — General Ledger, Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, and Fixed Assets.
* Integrated CRM, order management, and e-commerce functionality.
* Warehousing and inventory management.
* Manufacturing and material requirements planning (MRP).
* Product and catalog management with pricing, promotions, and supply-chain fulfillment.

## Project Status

Apache OFBiz has no open-core split — everything is Apache-2.0. It is a mature volunteer project with a smaller community and slower velocity than ERPNext or Odoo.
