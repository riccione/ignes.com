## Key Features

* CRM with pipeline, leads, opportunities, and sales team management.
* Integrated eCommerce store and website builder.
* Invoicing, payments, bank reconciliation, and financial reporting.
* Inventory, warehousing, barcode, and replenishment.
* Point of Sale for retail and restaurants with multi-store support.

## Licensing

Odoo is open-core. Odoo Community Edition is LGPL-3.0 and free to self-host. Odoo Enterprise is proprietary and adds modules such as full Accounting, Studio, Payroll, Helpdesk, and Planning via a per-user subscription.
