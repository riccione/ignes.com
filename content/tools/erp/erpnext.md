## Key Features

* Accounting — multi-subsidiary, multi-currency, general ledger, payables and receivables, financial statements.
* Procurement — procure-to-pay cycle with material requests, purchase orders, and multi-level approvals.
* Sales — order-to-cash with sales orders, invoices, pricing rules, and payments.
* Stock — warehouses, serial and batch tracking, stock ledger, and inventory reports.
* Manufacturing — multi-level BOM, production planning, work orders, subcontracting, and quality checks.

## Licensing

ERPNext has no feature gating: the complete codebase is GPL-3.0. Frappe's paid offerings cover hosting, support, and services, not additional ERP features.
