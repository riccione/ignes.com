## Key Features

* Over 100 professionally made brushes preloaded, plus 9+ unique brush engines.
* Brush stabilizers — three different ways to smooth and stabilize your strokes.
* Simple and powerful 2D animation with onion skinning, tweening, and video export.
* Full color management via LCMS (ICC) and OpenColorIO (EXR) for HDR painting.
* PSD support — opens PSD files that even Photoshop cannot — plus Python scripting.
