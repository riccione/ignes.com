## Key Features

* Familiar by design — menus, shortcuts, panels, and tools where your hands expect them.
* Native and fast — GPU compositor on wgpu with copy-on-write tiles, no Electron.
* Real PSD files — open, edit, and save layered Photoshop documents.
* Non-destructive editing — adjustment layers, smart objects, and 70+ live-preview filters.
* 8/16/32-bit documents in RGB, CMYK, Lab, and Grayscale with ICC color management.
