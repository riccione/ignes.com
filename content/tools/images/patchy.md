## Key Features

* Layered Photoshop files with editable text, groups, masks, clipping, blend modes, and layer styles.
* Pixel art and game asset workflows — fixed palettes, sprite sheets, seamless tiling, DDS textures, and indexed formats.
* Layered import from Affinity, Krita, GIMP, OpenRaster, Paint.NET, Aseprite, and layered TIFF.
* Non-destructive editing with adjustment layers, Smart Objects, and editable Smart Filters.
* G'MIC and GEGL filters, classic Photoshop plug-ins, scripting, batch/CLI, and camera raw support.

## Project Status

Patchy is young and effectively developed by a single maintainer, though releases are frequent. macOS builds are Apple Silicon only, and the browser build omits G'MIC, GEGL filters, plug-ins, and printing.
