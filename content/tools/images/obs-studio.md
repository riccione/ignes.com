## Key Features

* High performance real-time video/audio capturing and compositing with unlimited scenes.
* Intuitive audio mixer with per-source filters and VST plugin support.
* Customizable transitions, stinger videos, and Studio Mode for previewing before going live.
* Multiview for monitoring and switching between up to 8 scenes simultaneously.
