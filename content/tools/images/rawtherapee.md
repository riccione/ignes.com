## Key Features

* Non-destructive, 32-bit floating point processing engine with modern demosaicing algorithms.
* Outstanding raw support — pixel-shift compositing, dark frame subtraction, flat field correction.
* Best-in-class demosaicing, including High Dynamic Range DNG support.
* Cross-platform and available in over 15 languages.
