## Key Features

* Powerful curve-based keyframe animations for position, scale, rotation, and opacity.
* Unlimited tracks/layers with drag-and-drop timeline editing and frame-accurate controls.
* Video transitions with real-time previews, compositing, and image overlays.
* 3D animated titles and effects, title templates, and SVG vector support.
