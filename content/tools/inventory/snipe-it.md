## Key Features

* IT asset management for devices, accessories, components, and consumables.
* License management.
* Check-in/check-out with asset history and audit trail.
* Barcode and QR labels with standard scanner support.
* REST API with LDAP/Active Directory sync, 2FA, and HTTPS-only cookies.
