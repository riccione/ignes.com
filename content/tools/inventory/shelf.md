## Key Features

* Asset inventory with a page per asset: history, custodian, location, and QR code.
* Bookings and availability timeline that prevent double-booking.
* Check-in/check-out and custody tracking.
* Kits that bundle assets into one bookable unit.
* Labels and barcodes, plus the Shelf Companion mobile app for scanning and audits.
