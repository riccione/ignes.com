## Key Features

* Organize parts into structured categories.
* Manage suppliers and supplier items.
* Instant stock knowledge by part, location, or build.
* Bill of Materials (BOM) management.
* Extensible via REST API and plugins, with an official mobile app for barcode stock actions.
