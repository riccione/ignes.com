## Key Features

* Robust CKEditor-based WYSIWYG editor with powerful wiki syntax.
* Strong rights management and advanced search.
* Over 900 extensions, including App Within Minutes for building apps without dev mode.
* Deployable anywhere with JDK 1.8+ — .deb packages, Docker, or standalone Jetty.
