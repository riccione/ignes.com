## Key Features

* Saves and restores only used blocks — multicast restore at 8 GB/min on large clusters.
* Broad filesystem support: ext4, xfs, btrfs, NTFS, HFS+, APFS, FAT, exFAT, and more.
* Clone 40+ computers simultaneously with Clonezilla lite server via PXE and Wake-on-LAN.
* AES-256 image encryption with storage on local disk, ssh, samba, NFS, or WebDAV.
* MBR and GPT, BIOS or UEFI, unattended mode, LVM2 and LUKS support.
