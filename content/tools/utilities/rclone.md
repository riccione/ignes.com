## Key Features

* Sync (one-way), Bisync (two-way), and Copy modes with MD5/SHA-1 integrity checks at all times.
* Optional end-to-end encryption, compression, and chunking backends (Crypt, Compress, Chunker).
* Mount cloud storage as a local disk and serve it over HTTP, WebDAV, FTP, SFTP, or DLNA.
* 70+ supported providers with server-side cloud-to-cloud transfers that skip your local disk.
