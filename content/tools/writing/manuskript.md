## Key Features

* Outliner and index cards for hierarchical organisation.
* Snowflake-method novel assistant to grow a premise into a full story.
* Characters, plots, world-building, and item tracking.
* Distraction-free mode with word-count or time goals.
* Import and export HTML, ePub, OpenDocument, and DocX, with spell checking and frequency analysis.

## Project Status

Manuskript is still in development with a slow release cadence; expect limited feature velocity, though bug-fix releases continue.
