## Key Features

* Hide-away, distraction-free fullscreen interface.
* Custom themes controlling font, colors, and background image.
* On-the-fly updating statistics and daily goals.
* Multiple open documents and spell checking.
* Auto-loads your last work and cursor position, with a portable mode via a Data folder.
